Your notes are sent to Google

When you start a banter, the note text (or your selection) is sent to the Gemini API and handled under Google's terms and privacy policy. Do not use the plugin on notes you must not share with a third party.

AI output can be wrong or uncomfortable

The banter is generated by AI. It may be inaccurate, misread your note, or feel harsh — the devil teases on purpose. It is entertainment, not professional advice (medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or otherwise).

Your notes are only changed when you ask

The plugin writes to a note only when you press “Save to end of note” (with memory on, it also writes to the memory folder). Keep backups of your vault as usual.

Third-party services may change

Gemini models, voices, pricing, and APIs can change or stop without notice, which may break features.

Network use & privacy

There is no server run by the author and no telemetry. The only destination is Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), and requests are sent only when you act.

What is sent, and when
  • When you start a banter: the note text (or your selection). Frontmatter (YAML) and previously saved banter blocks are removed first.
  • When you reply: the note, the banter, and your reply are sent once more.
  • With memory on (off by default): each character's latest memos about notes of the same kind (up to 10 each) and current impression are sent with the note. history.md is never sent. Memos and impressions are written to your vault as soon as a banter is shown.
  • When you use read aloud (off by default): the generated banter text is sent to Gemini TTS. Audio is kept in memory only and never saved to a file.
  • When you press “Check key”: one request to check that the key works.

How Google handles what you send is up to Google's terms. Under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service, content sent through the free tier (Unpaid Services) may be used to improve Google's products. If that matters to you, read the terms and use a key from a paid tier (a project with billing enabled).

API key handling

Where it is stored

  • Obsidian 1.11.4 or later: in Obsidian's secret storage (SecretStorage). It lives outside your vault files, so vault sync, backups, and sharing do not include the key.
  • Older Obsidian: in plain text in the plugin's data.json (<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/angel-and-devil/data.json). Take care when you sync or share the vault. Once you open the vault in Obsidian 1.11.4 or later, the key moves to secret storage and is removed from data.json.

What secret storage cannot do

Secret storage is not something this plugin built; it is a feature Obsidian itself offers to every plugin. It cannot hide the key from other plugins. Installed plugins can read secrets, and Obsidian plugins are not isolated from each other. Only install plugins you trust.

What this plugin does and does not do

  • The key is sent only to the Gemini API.
  • The settings never show the saved key again (only its last 4 characters). You can remove it any time with Delete.
  • The key never appears in logs, error messages, or notices.

If you think it leaked

Delete the key in Google AI Studio and create a new one. With a dedicated key, you can replace it without affecting anything else.
